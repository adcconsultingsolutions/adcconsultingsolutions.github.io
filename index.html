Who We Are

About ADC Consulting Solutions

ADC Consulting Solutions provides professional consulting grounded in 20+ years of frontline and leadership expertise across human services, housing, education, and nonprofit sectors.

Our work is rooted in both operational excellence and a deep commitment to the communities we serve. We partner with agencies, government entities, nonprofits, and community partners to build and strengthen programs that are compliant, sustainable, and centered around the people they serve.

We specialize in:

  • Program design and redesign
  • Contract compliance and quality assurance
  • Workforce development and training
  • Program evaluation and performance management
  • HUD HQS and NSPIRE inspections
  • Licensed social work services
  • Special education consulting (K–12)

Our Team

Leadership

Antonia D. Coleman, LSW, CPPM
Founder & Principal Consultant

Antonia D. Coleman is a senior human services and housing leader with more than 20 years of experience across nonprofit, municipal, and public housing authority environments. Her path into this work was not accidental. As a young mother who worked through college, Antonia found herself alongside people navigating the same systems she was studying. She saw firsthand that access to resources mattered, but it was the knowledge and genuine commitment of the person delivering the service that made the lasting difference.

Her brother's experience with special needs drew her into special education, where she learned early that meeting people where they are, and knowing how to serve them well, is not a nicety. It is the foundation of effective practice. That conviction has carried through every role she has held since, from direct service to executive leadership.

Antonia founded ADC Consulting Solutions on the belief that strong programs, built by professionals who truly know the work, change lives and communities. Every engagement reflects that standard.

Antonia D. Coleman, LSW, CPPM
