Who We Are

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

ADC Consulting Solutions is a mission-driven consulting practice dedicated to strengthening organizations that serve people. We bring 20+ years of frontline and leadership expertise across human services and housing to help agencies, government entities, nonprofits, and community partners build programs that are compliant, sustainable, and centered around the people they serve. We listen first, tailor every solution to your specific needs, and measure our success by your outcomes.

Our Vision

Our vision is a future where every individual and family, including those facing barriers to stability, has access to the services, support, and opportunities they need to thrive. We partner with organizations that share this commitment, equipping them with the tools, knowledge, and strategic support to turn that vision into measurable community impact. We believe strong programs, led by knowledgeable practitioners, change lives.

Our Three Core Commitments
01

Client-Centered Practice

Your priorities drive our work. We build solutions around your organizational strengths, your population, and your mission because no two clients are the same.

02

Results-Focused Delivery

We set clear goals, track progress, and hold ourselves accountable to measurable outcomes at every stage of every engagement.

03

Community Impact

We measure success not just by program metrics, but by the stability, safety, and opportunity created for the residents and communities you serve.

Leadership

Antonia D. Coleman
Founder & Principal Consultant · LSW, CPPM

Antonia D. Coleman is a senior human services and housing leader with more than 20 years of experience across nonprofit, municipal, and public housing authority environments. Her path into this work was not accidental. As a young mother who worked through college, Antonia found herself alongside people navigating the same systems she was studying. She saw firsthand that access to resources mattered, but it was the knowledge and genuine commitment of the person delivering the service that made the lasting difference.

Her brother's experience with special needs drew her into special education, where she learned early that meeting people where they are, and knowing how to serve them well, is not a nicety. It is the foundation of effective practice. That conviction has carried through every role she has held since, from direct service to executive leadership.

Antonia founded ADC Consulting Solutions on the belief that strong programs, built by practitioners who truly know the work, change lives and communities. Every engagement reflects that standard.
