Who We Are

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

ADC Consulting Solutions LLC is a mission-driven consulting practice dedicated to expanding access to quality, affordable housing through expert guidance, strategic partnership, and ethical practice. Drawing on 20+ years of housing expertise — as a Licensed Social Worker, Certified HUD HQS Inspector, and NSPIRE-certified professional — we bring a rare combination of operational knowledge, compliance proficiency, and community insight to every engagement. Our clients are at the center of everything we do — we listen first, tailor every solution to your specific needs, and measure our success by your outcomes.

Our Vision

Our vision is a future where every individual and family — including those facing barriers to stability — has access to safe, stable, and affordable housing. We partner with organizations that share this commitment, equipping them with the tools, knowledge, and strategic support to turn that vision into measurable community impact.

Our Three Core Commitments
01

Client-Centered Practice

Your priorities drive our work. We build solutions around your organizational strengths, your population, and your mission — because no two clients are the same.

02

Results-Focused Delivery

We set clear goals, track progress, and hold ourselves accountable to measurable outcomes at every stage of every engagement.

03

Community Impact

We measure success not just by program metrics, but by the stability, safety, and opportunity created for the residents and communities you serve.
